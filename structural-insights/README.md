# Structural Insights

This folder contains current interpretive notes that apply the upstream structural framework to familiar contemporary technical and philosophical concepts.

These notes are not part of the primary engineering evidence chain and are not historical provenance. Their role is to provide readable bridges from familiar terminology and arguments into the structural distinctions developed across the broader research program.

Current notes:

- [Structural Retyping of Contemporary AI Concepts](./structural-retyping-of-contemporary-ai-concepts.md)
- [Simulation Is Not an Ontology](./simulation-is-not-an-ontology.md)

The guiding principle is simple:

> begin with the reader’s familiar vocabulary or argument, then make the underlying structural distinctions explicit.

Where relevant, these notes point downstream toward the patent architecture, deterministic execution-control implementation, formal Lean work, and other inspectable artifacts in the repository.
